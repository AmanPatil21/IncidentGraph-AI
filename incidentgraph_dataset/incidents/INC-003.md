# INC-003: Invoice export stuck

Incident ID: INC-003
Date: 2026-10-03
Service: invoice-exporter
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-355
Error code: S3_ACCESS_DENIED
Deployment: DEP-102 (v2.10.1) at 2026-10-03 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The invoice-exporter service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-03 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-355 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for invoice-exporter showed S3_ACCESS_DENIED. Deployment DEP-102 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Service account IAM policy update removed object write permission for invoice export bucket.

## Impact
194 affected operations were delayed for 24 minutes.

## Resolution
Compare policy revisions; request least-privilege permission restoration; replay failed exports after approval. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-IAM-003 and postmortem PM-003.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for iam policy regression and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
