# INC-006: Fraud scoring service errors

Incident ID: INC-006
Date: 2026-10-06
Service: fraud-scoring
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-467
Error code: MODEL_ARTIFACT_NOT_FOUND
Deployment: DEP-105 (v2.13.1) at 2026-10-06 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The fraud-scoring service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-06 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-467 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for fraud-scoring showed MODEL_ARTIFACT_NOT_FOUND. Deployment DEP-105 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
New fraud model deployment referenced an artifact path that was not published.

## Impact
305 affected operations were delayed for 33 minutes.

## Resolution
Validate artifact registry path; roll back model pointer after approval. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-ML-006 and postmortem PM-006.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for missing model artifact and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
