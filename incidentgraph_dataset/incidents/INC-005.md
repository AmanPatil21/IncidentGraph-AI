# INC-005: Customer notification backlog

Incident ID: INC-005
Date: 2026-10-05
Service: notification-worker
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-438
Error code: QUEUE_CONSUMER_LAG
Deployment: DEP-104 (v2.12.1) at 2026-10-05 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The notification-worker service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-05 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-438 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for notification-worker showed QUEUE_CONSUMER_LAG. Deployment DEP-104 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Notification consumer replicas dropped from 6 to 1 after autoscaler configuration change.

## Impact
268 affected operations were delayed for 30 minutes.

## Resolution
Inspect HPA settings; restore approved replica minimum; verify queue drains. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-QUEUE-005 and postmortem PM-005.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for insufficient queue consumers and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
