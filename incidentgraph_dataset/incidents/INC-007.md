# INC-007: Merchant payout delay

Incident ID: INC-007
Date: 2026-10-07
Service: payout-service
Severity: SEV-1
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-501
Error code: RATE_LIMIT_429
Deployment: DEP-106 (v2.14.1) at 2026-10-07 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The payout-service service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-07 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-501 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for payout-service showed RATE_LIMIT_429. Deployment DEP-106 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Partner bank API rate limit was exceeded by a new unbounded polling loop.

## Impact
342 affected operations were delayed for 36 minutes.

## Resolution
Throttle polling; implement exponential backoff; coordinate with partner. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-RATE-007 and postmortem PM-007.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for partner api rate limiting and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
