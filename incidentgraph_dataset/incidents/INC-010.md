# INC-010: Account ledger write errors

Incident ID: INC-010
Date: 2026-10-10
Service: ledger-api
Severity: SEV-1
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-620
Error code: DB_DEADLOCK
Deployment: DEP-109 (v2.17.1) at 2026-10-10 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The ledger-api service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-10 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-620 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for ledger-api showed DB_DEADLOCK. Deployment DEP-109 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Concurrent ledger updates acquired account locks in inconsistent order.

## Impact
453 affected operations were delayed for 45 minutes.

## Resolution
Review transaction ordering; add bounded retry; test locking change. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-LOCK-010 and postmortem PM-010.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for database deadlocks and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
