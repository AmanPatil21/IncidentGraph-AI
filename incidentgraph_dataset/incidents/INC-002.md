# INC-002: Payment authorization timeout spike

Incident ID: INC-002
Date: 2026-10-02
Service: payment-api
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-310
Error code: UPSTREAM_GATEWAY_TIMEOUT
Deployment: DEP-101 (v2.9.1) at 2026-10-02 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The payment-api service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-02 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-310 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for payment-api showed UPSTREAM_GATEWAY_TIMEOUT. Deployment DEP-101 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
External gateway latency exceeded the configured 2-second timeout during a provider degradation.

## Impact
157 affected operations were delayed for 21 minutes.

## Resolution
Verify gateway status; enable approved failover route; monitor retry volume. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-PAY-002 and postmortem PM-002.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for gateway latency degradation and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
