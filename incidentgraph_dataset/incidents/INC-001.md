# INC-001: Settlement batch failed

Incident ID: INC-001
Date: 2026-10-01
Service: settlement-worker
Severity: SEV-1
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-284
Error code: DB_CONNECTION_POOL_EXHAUSTED
Deployment: DEP-100 (v2.8.1) at 2026-10-01 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The settlement-worker service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-01 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-284 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for settlement-worker showed DB_CONNECTION_POOL_EXHAUSTED. Deployment DEP-100 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Database connection pool exhausted due to a connection leak introduced in settlement-worker v2.8.1.

## Impact
120 affected operations were delayed for 18 minutes.

## Resolution
Inspect active sessions and pool metrics; patch connection handling; restart worker only after approval. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-DB-001 and postmortem PM-001.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for db connection pool saturation and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
