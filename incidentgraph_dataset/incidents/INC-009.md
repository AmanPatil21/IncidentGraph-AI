# INC-009: Webhook delivery failures

Incident ID: INC-009
Date: 2026-10-09
Service: webhook-dispatcher
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-589
Error code: TLS_CERT_EXPIRED
Deployment: DEP-108 (v2.16.1) at 2026-10-09 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The webhook-dispatcher service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-09 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-589 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for webhook-dispatcher showed TLS_CERT_EXPIRED. Deployment DEP-108 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Outbound webhook signing proxy certificate expired at 00:00 UTC.

## Impact
416 affected operations were delayed for 42 minutes.

## Resolution
Rotate certificate; validate trust chain; replay queued deliveries with approval. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-TLS-009 and postmortem PM-009.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for expired tls certificate and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
