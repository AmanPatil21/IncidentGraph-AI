# INC-008: Order analytics dashboard stale

Incident ID: INC-008
Date: 2026-10-08
Service: analytics-etl
Severity: SEV-2
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-553
Error code: UPSTREAM_DEPENDENCY_FAILED
Deployment: DEP-107 (v2.15.1) at 2026-10-08 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The analytics-etl service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-08 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-553 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for analytics-etl showed UPSTREAM_DEPENDENCY_FAILED. Deployment DEP-107 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Analytics ETL skipped because prerequisite JOB-552 failed on missing source partition.

## Impact
379 affected operations were delayed for 39 minutes.

## Resolution
Restore missing partition; validate dependency chain; rerun ETL after approval. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-ETL-008 and postmortem PM-008.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for missing upstream partition and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
