# INC-004: Nightly reconciliation mismatch

Incident ID: INC-004
Date: 2026-10-04
Service: reconciliation
Severity: SEV-1
Status: Resolved
Primary job: JOB-402
Error code: SCHEMA_MISMATCH
Deployment: DEP-103 (v2.11.1) at 2026-10-04 08:57 UTC

## Symptoms
The reconciliation service experienced elevated failures beginning at 2026-10-04 09:03 UTC. The primary job JOB-402 failed at 09:07 UTC, delaying dependent operations.

## Investigation evidence
At 09:03 UTC, logs for reconciliation showed SCHEMA_MISMATCH. Deployment DEP-103 had completed six minutes earlier. An operator correlated deployment and error telemetry and reviewed the dependency graph.

## Confirmed root cause
Upstream transactions added currency_code_v2 while reconciliation expected currency_code.

## Impact
231 affected operations were delayed for 27 minutes.

## Resolution
Review schema contract; deploy backward-compatible mapping; rerun validation. The team verified recovery by checking error rate and successful job completion.

## Related knowledge
See runbook RB-SCHEMA-004 and postmortem PM-004.

## Lessons learned
Add automated regression checks for schema compatibility mismatch and alert on the leading indicator before scheduled downstream jobs begin.
