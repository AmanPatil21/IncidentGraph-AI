# Postmortem PM-005: Customer notification backlog

Incident: INC-005
Date: 2026-10-05

## Timeline (UTC)
- 08:57 Deployment DEP-104 (v2.12.1) completed.
- 09:03 QUEUE_CONSUMER_LAG appeared in notification-worker logs.
- 09:07 Job JOB-438 failed.
- 09:15 Alert fired.
- 09:22 Investigation identified the contributing failure.

## Root cause
Notification consumer replicas dropped from 6 to 1 after autoscaler configuration change.

## Corrective action
Inspect HPA settings; restore approved replica minimum; verify queue drains.

## Prevention
Introduce regression tests and service-level alerts for insufficient queue consumers.
